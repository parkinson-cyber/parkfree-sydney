Rates & Guidelines

North Sydney has two types of meters located throughout the Local Government Area (LGA) - Pay & Go meters and the Pay & Display meters. All North Sydney meters accept payment by coin, credit card and mobile phone

 

Meter Rates

Parking meter rates throughout the North Sydney Council area vary depending on the time and location. Peak hours are 8.30am - 6pm and off-peak hours are 6pm -12 midnight.

Data gathered from regular traffic studies and parking surveys have enabled Council to determine four "Parking Demand Areas" within the parking meter zones. The Parking Demand areas are:

  1. High - North Sydney CBD and Alfred Street South - 'Park & Swim Scheme' Pay & Display meters.
  2. Med-High - Crows Nest, St Leonards CBD areas and North Sydney CBD shoulder area.
  3. Low-Med - Neutral Bay CBD & fringe areas.
  4. Low - low demand area and some long term parking.

Regulations & Guidelines

Before using a parking meter motorists should always check parking signposts located close to all meters to confirm pay parking hours in the area. Street signs take priority over parking information displayed on meters (for example, in the event of Special Event Clearways and road closures).

The following regulations outline the rules for parking in NSW:

  1. The Australian Road Rules 2008
  2. The Road Transport (Safety and Traffic Management) (Road Rules) Regulation 1999

Mobility Parking Scheme

Vehicles displaying a Mobility Parking Scheme Authority can park in metered or ticketed parking areas without paying for parking. For more information on the Mobility Parking Scheme visit the RTA's website.
