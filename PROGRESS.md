## Changelog

- **2026-09-10 (meter apps assessed, declined)** — Checked whether meter-payment apps could supply meter/bay data. Park'nPay is NSW Government but has **no published API** (data.nsw has only its procurement contract); City of Ryde's in-ground bay sensors feed it and are **not** published; City of Sydney has no live meter-status service. Decision recorded in `scripts/DATA-SOURCE-LEADS.md`: **don't scrape their private backends** — same call this project already made on Parkopedia, and a scraped app backend would break without notice and take the map's accuracy with it. Legitimate route noted: a formal data request to Digital NSW and to City of Ryde. Also logged **M8** in PLAN.md — the requested iOS-native/glass visual pass, not started.

- **2026-09-10 (supervision moved off the laptop + live status page)** — The local hourly task only runs while the desktop app is open, and it had fired **once in 35 hours**. Supervision now runs in GitHub Actions instead: `.github/workflows/supervise.yml` (daily 05:20 Sydney) re-scrapes the council car-park pages and **opens a PR** if a council changed something — never pushes to main, because a changed parking rule is something a person should read — after a guard that refuses a diff which halves the free periods (that shape means a failed fetch, not a real change) plus typecheck and tests; a second job opens/closes a single issue if the live feed passes 6 h old. **Live status page at `/status.html`**, built hourly by `scripts/build-status.mjs` from the repo and the feed (never hand-written) and published to `data-live`: coverage %, per-suburb bars, feed health, banked calibration readings, whether crowd storage is shared, and recent work. Checkable from a phone, and safe to make public.

- **2026-09-10 (busy estimate)** — **How busy is it, and how much should you trust that.** `src/lib/availability.ts` scores P(a space is free) from, in order: the law (a ban is certainty, not an estimate — and an unknown street gets *no* estimate rather than a guess); crowd reports on that street decayed by age (half-life 10 min for "someone left", 20 min for "it's full"); **measured** occupancy from the nearest TfNSW Park&Ride within 1.2 km — the only hard number available; and a time-of-day prior that is always labelled "typical pattern, not a measurement". Confidence is capped at 0.85: without a sensor in the kerb, certainty would be a lie. The sheet (`BusySheet.tsx`) shows headline, a confidence pill and the full evidence checklist, so a driver can overrule it. 21 new tests (143 total) pin the invariants: the law always wins, reports fade, a pile-on can't reach certainty, a car park 9 km away is not evidence. Verified live: Ashfield P&R at 100% pulls nearby College St to 38% at 45% confidence.
- **2026-09-10 (calibration data)** — the hourly job now **accumulates** occupancy to `history.jsonl` on `data-live` instead of overwriting (40 rows/hour, trimmed to 400k). This is the only ground truth the project has for how demand actually moves, and is what the hand-set priors should be replaced with once there is enough of it.

- **2026-09-10 (Northern Beaches / Manly)** — The LGA had **zero** coverage; it now has 1,408 segments. Northern Beaches publishes its road-sign asset register (`maps.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au` `Assets/MapServer/4`, 29,691 signs, ~9,800 parking) with the RTA sign *type* in `NARR1` — so the time limit is real (a "Parking (2 hours)" sign is 2P) but the **hours are not published** (time plates are separate records with no times on them). Limits are therefore stored with no interval and the app says "2P", never an invented "2P 8am–6pm"; that under-promises, which is the safe direction, and a photographed sign overrides it. **Manly went 0% → 55% classified** (189 streets with a time limit). Sign-density rule matters: one sign governs at most 120 m of ban / 200 m of time limit, so a segment is only tagged if its signs could plausibly cover its length — without it, a single No Parking sign blacked out all 3.8 km of Cottage Point Road; 197 segments stay unknown for this reason. Classified 12,333 → 13,741 (+11%).
