- **2026-09-10 (busy estimate)** — **How busy is it, and how much should you trust that.** `src/lib/availability.ts` scores P(a space is free) from, in order: the law (a ban is certainty, not an estimate — and an unknown street gets *no* estimate rather than a guess); crowd reports on that street decayed by age (half-life 10 min for "someone left", 20 min for "it's full"); **measured** occupancy from the nearest TfNSW Park&Ride within 1.2 km — the only hard number available; and a time-of-day prior that is always labelled "typical pattern, not a measurement". Confidence is capped at 0.85: without a sensor in the kerb, certainty would be a lie.
