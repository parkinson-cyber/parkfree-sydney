ParkFree Sydney

Always check the sign on the kerb. ParkFree is built from published council and NSW Government data. Signs change, roadworks happen, and data has gaps. The sign wins, every time.

What the colours mean

  • Free now — no restriction applies at this moment.
  • Free, time-limited — free, but there's a maximum stay (2P, 4P…).
  • Paid now — a meter or ticket applies right now.
  • Residents only — permit-holder parking.
  • No parking now — clearway, no stopping, or no parking.
  • No confirmed rules — we have no published data for this kerb. It is not a claim that parking is free.

Common questions

A street is the wrong colour. That's worth reporting — it's how the map improves. Open an issue with the street name and suburb, and a photo of the sign if you have one. A photographed sign outranks everything else in the data.

Why is so much of Sydney grey? Because most councils don't publish their parking signs. Roughly a fifth of Sydney's streets have confirmed rules. We would rather show grey than guess — a wrong "free" is how you get a fine.

Why does the app say "estimate"? How busy a street is can't be measured without a sensor in the kerb. Where we show a percentage it's an estimate with a confidence level and the evidence behind it, never a fact.

My crowd report didn't appear for anyone else. Reports expire quickly by design — 15 minutes for "someone left", 30 for "it's full" — because old parking information is worse than none.

Do I need an account? No. There's nothing to sign up for.
