Privacy

ParkFree Sydney · last updated 21 September 2026

The short version. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your location stays on your phone unless you tap the report button. Where you parked is stored only on your device.

Your location

ParkFree asks for location only while you are using the app, and only to do two things: centre the map on you, and — if you tap the report button — work out which street you are standing on so the report lands on the right kerb.

Your position is not sent anywhere while you are simply looking at the map. It is not logged, not stored on a server, and not shared. If you decline the permission the app still works; it just won't know where you are.

Crowd reports

When you report a spot as free or full, the app sends the report's location (snapped to the nearest street), what you reported, and the time. It also sends an anonymous identifier generated on your device at install, used only to rate-limit abuse (30 reports per device per hour).

  • That identifier is a random value. It is not your Apple ID, your advertising ID, your phone number or your email, and it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Reports expire automatically — 15 minutes for "someone left", 30 minutes for "it's full" — and are deleted when they expire.
  • Reports are not attributed to anyone. Nobody, including us, can see who reported what.

Where you parked, and your timer

"Park here" and the parking timer are stored only on your phone (in the app's local storage) and cleared after 24 hours. They are never uploaded. Timer reminders are local notifications generated on the device.

What we never collect

  • No account, email address, or name — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera roll access.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, no analytics.
  • No location history, no trip tracking, no background location.

Services the app talks to

  • OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles — map tiles. Your device requests the tiles for the area you are viewing.
  • GitHub — the live car-park occupancy feed is a static file fetched from the project's public repository.
  • The ParkFree API (hosted on Vercel) — only for crowd reports, as described above.

Each of these sees your IP address the way any website you visit does. We do not combine that with anything else, and we keep no server logs of your browsing of the map.

Parking data itself

The map's rules come from published council and NSW Government data, sign registers, and photographs of signs. Sources are listed in the open-source repository. A sign on the street always beats this app.

Children

ParkFree collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the previous version stays in the repository's history. Questions, or want a report removed? Open an issue at github.com/parkinson-cyber/parkfree-sydney.
